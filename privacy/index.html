Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Pause ("the app," "we," "us") helps you use your selected apps more intentionally. We care about your privacy. Most of the app works entirely on your device, and we do not run our own servers or sell your personal information. The free version of Pause shows ads through Google AdMob; you can remove ads by subscribing to Pause Premium.

Information and how it's handled

Sign in with Apple. When you sign in, Apple provides a unique identifier for your account and, if you choose to share it, your name. This is stored only in the secure Keychain on your device and is used solely to sign you in. We do not request your email address, and we do not operate servers that receive this information.

Apps you choose to pause (Screen Time data). Pause uses Apple's Screen Time / Family Controls framework so you can select the apps and categories you want to put behind a puzzle. These selections are represented by private tokens that remain on your device. Apple does not reveal the identity of the apps you select to us, and your selections are never transmitted off your device.

App settings and subscription status. Your preferences (such as which puzzles are enabled and your subscription status) are stored on your device and, where applicable, in your private iCloud account provided by Apple. We do not have access to this data.

Purchases. Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles all payment information; we never receive or store your payment details. We only receive the subscription entitlement status needed to unlock premium features.

Advertising (free version only). The free version of Pause displays ads using Google AdMob, a service provided by Google. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process certain information, including device identifiers, IP address, approximate (coarse) location derived from your IP address, device characteristics, and information about your interactions with ads. We have configured AdMob to serve non-personalized ads: your information is not used to build a personalized advertising profile, and we do not use Apple's Advertising Identifier (IDFA) to track you across other companies' apps and websites. AdMob still uses limited data to select contextual ads, cap how often you see an ad, measure performance, and prevent fraud and abuse. Google's use of this information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. Subscribing to Pause Premium removes all ads.

Website analytics (try-pause.com)

This section covers try-pause.com specifically — it does not apply to the app, which is covered above. With your consent (via the cookie banner shown on your first visit), our website uses Google Analytics and PostHog to understand how visitors use the site: pages viewed, general location (country/region-level, derived from IP address), device/browser type, referring site, and on-site interactions such as clicks and scrolling (including anonymized session recordings via PostHog). This helps us understand which content is useful and where the site could be clearer — it is not used to build advertising profiles and is not sold. You can decline this tracking in the cookie banner, and your choice is remembered on that browser. Google's and PostHog's use of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and the PostHog Privacy Policy.

What we do and don't do

  • We do not operate our own servers or collect your personal data on them.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information.
  • The app itself does not use analytics or third-party trackers beyond Google AdMob (free version only). Our website uses cookie-based analytics as described above, with your consent.

Your choices

  • Remove ads by subscribing to Pause Premium.
  • You can reset your device's advertising identifier or limit ad tracking in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security.
  • Where required by law (for example, in the EEA and UK), we will ask for your consent before showing ads.
  • On try-pause.com, you can change your cookie choice anytime: manage cookie preferences.

Data retention and deletion

Your in-app data is stored on your device and your private iCloud. You can remove it anytime by signing out in the app or deleting the app.

Children's privacy

Pause is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Apple's services

Pause relies on Apple frameworks (Sign in with Apple, Screen Time / Family Controls, iCloud, and the App Store), which are governed by Apple's own Privacy Policy.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted here with an updated "Last updated" date.
